## Dancing turn and opening port de bras

The moving turn uses `pirouette.webp` for eight principal poses and
`pirouette-inbetweens.webp` for the eight passing poses. The sparse neutral
turnaround above provides the standing handoffs. `port-de-bras.webp`
supplies eight additional painted arm positions for the two changes of reach
in the preparation.

The supporting foot takes the weight in a small plié. The arms open toward
the audience's right before sweeping in and turning left. The eyes remain
on the audience while the shoulders pass through the first profile. Draw a
brief, genuine rear silhouette with foreshortened flukes overlapping the
skirt. Draw the head already finding the audience again as the shoulders
emerge from the opposite profile. Open the arms and let the hair, ribbons
and hem follow after the supporting foot has stopped turning. Standing
scale, heel marks, face identity, and daylight match the walking artwork.
Paint hands and fingers individually through the two arm sweeps, including
the moment both round in front of the waist.

Reference for the preparation and spotting is Northern Ballet's
[demonstration of the pirouette](https://www.northernballet.com/index.php/the-moves).
The arm collection and controlled release follow the
[Royal Ballet demonstration by Akane Takada](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88bsYB9i6Mc).
